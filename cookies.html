Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Through Design Journal uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Last updated: 12 March 2025 Effective: 1 April 2025 Version: 2.1

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Cookies help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your overall reading experience.

We also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. In this policy, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies."

2. How we use cookies

Through Design Journal uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To ensure the website works correctly, including navigation, form submissions, and secure areas.
  • Preferences: To remember your settings, such as language or region, and to provide a more personalised experience.
  • Analytics & performance: To understand how visitors interact with our site, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve our editorial content.
  • Marketing & advertising: We do not run third-party ads, but we may use limited cookies to measure the reach of our newsletter or social media campaigns.

3. Types of cookies we use

Below is a detailed list of the cookies we currently use on Through Design Journal. We categorise them by their purpose and duration.

Category Cookie name Purpose Duration
Essential td_session Maintains your session while you browse the site, ensuring forms and navigation work correctly. Session
Essential td_csrf Security token to protect against cross-site request forgery. 1 hour
Preferences td_prefs Stores your display preferences (e.g., font size, contrast) and consent choices. 12 months
Analytics _ga Google Analytics — distinguishes unique users and tracks page views anonymously. 2 years
Analytics _gid Google Analytics — used to distinguish users for reporting purposes. 24 hours
Analytics _gat Google Analytics — throttles request rate to improve performance. 1 minute
Marketing _fbp Used by Meta (Facebook) to measure the effectiveness of our social media campaigns. 3 months
Note: We do not use any advertising cookies that track you across other websites, nor do we sell your data. The marketing cookies we use are strictly for measuring our own outreach and are only set with your consent.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We use the following third parties:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymous traffic analysis. Their privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Meta (Facebook): For campaign measurement. Their policy is at facebook.com/policy.php.
  • Mailchimp: If you subscribe to our newsletter, Mailchimp may set cookies to track email engagement. Their policy is at mailchimp.com/legal/privacy.

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies for more information.

5. Your choices & control

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Check your browser's help section for instructions (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  • Our consent banner: When you first visit our site, you can choose which categories of cookies to accept. You can change your mind at any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.
  • Opt-out tools: For Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
