Before you write

We love

  • Original reporting and deep-dive essays
  • Material studies with technical detail
  • Under-the-radar architectural projects
  • Thoughtful criticism of the built environment
  • Reader questions about design dilemmas

We don't publish

  • Sponsored posts or paid placements
  • Generic product round-ups
  • AI-generated articles
  • Press releases disguised as editorial
  • Content that hasn't been fact-checked
