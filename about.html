About Through Design

An independent editorial journal exploring the spaces we inhabit — and the ideas that shape them.

Our Story

Through Design began in 2019 as a shared notebook between two editors — one in Copenhagen, one in Milan — who were tired of the same glossy, surface-level design coverage. We wanted to write about the why behind spaces: the structural decisions, the material honesty, the quiet psychology of a well-planned room.

Today, we are a small, independent publication with a global readership. We publish long-form guides, critical essays, and practical knowledge for anyone who cares about the built environment — from architects and designers to homeowners and curious readers. We have no sponsored content, no pay-to-play features, and no trend-chasing. Just useful, considered writing.

“Design is not decoration. It is the thoughtful arrangement of space, light, and material to support how we actually live.”

What We Stand For

  • 01 Independence over influence. We do not accept sponsored posts, native advertising, or free products in exchange for coverage. Our recommendations are our own.
  • 02 Depth over speed. We publish fewer articles than most, but each one is researched, fact-checked, and written to last. No listicles, no clickbait.
  • 03 Materials matter. We believe the tactile reality of a space — the stone, the wood, the light — is as important as its form. We write about both.
  • 04 Accessible expertise. Our writing is rigorous but jargon-free. Whether you are a professional or a curious homeowner, you will find something useful here.

How We Work

Every article on Through Design goes through a three-stage editorial process: research (primary sources, site visits, and expert interviews), writing (by a named editor with subject expertise), and review (a second editor fact-checks and edits for clarity). We disclose any potential conflicts of interest at the bottom of relevant articles.

We are reader-supported. Our revenue comes from our optional newsletter subscriptions and from affiliate partnerships with independent bookshops and material suppliers — never from display advertising or sponsored content. If you value what we do, the best way to support us is to subscribe to our dispatch or share our work with a friend.

We are always happy to hear from readers. If you have a question, a correction, or a story idea, please get in touch.
